# DECIDAI Three-Minute Demo

## 0:00–0:30 — Problem and theme

Introduce DECIDAI: business AI needs to be explainable and accountable. The product principle is **AI Advises. Human Decides.**

## 0:30–1:15 — Create a case

Create the Laptop Procurement Request from `demo/README.md`. Explain that no supporting document is attached, so uncertainty is expected.

## 1:15–1:45 — Review the AI analysis

Show the AI Recommendation, AI Analysis Confidence, evidence, risk flags, missing information, and human review focus. Emphasize that a recommendation is advisory—not an automatic decision.

## 1:45–2:20 — Human Final Authority

Use the Human Final Authority panel. Enter a reviewer name and decision reason, review the confirmation dialog, and submit an explicit human final decision.

## 2:20–2:45 — Audit trail

Show Case Created, AI Analysis Completed, and Human Decision Submitted. If relevant, show the Human Override event.

## 2:45–3:00 — Dashboard and history

Return to the dashboard and Decision History to show the live outcome and recorded reviewer.

Close: **DECIDAI does not replace human judgment. It makes human judgment better informed, explainable, and auditable.**
