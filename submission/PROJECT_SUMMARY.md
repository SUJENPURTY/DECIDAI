# DECIDAI

## Tagline

AI Advises. Human Decides.

## Problem

Business teams increasingly use AI recommendations, but opaque or autonomous decision systems create accountability, trust, and governance risks.

## Solution

DECIDAI is a Human-in-the-Loop AI Decision Copilot that analyses business cases and supporting evidence, highlights risks and missing information, and provides an explainable recommendation while preserving final decision authority with a human reviewer.

## Innovation Framework

Input → Evidence Analysis → AI Recommendation → Explainability & Risk → Human Review → Human Final Decision → Audit Trail

## Tech Stack

- Frontend: React, Vite, Tailwind CSS
- Backend: FastAPI, Python
- AI: Google Gemini API
- Database: Supabase PostgreSQL
- Document processing: PDF, DOCX, TXT

## AI Capabilities

- Business case summarization
- Evidence analysis
- Recommendation generation
- Risk flag detection
- Missing-information detection
- Human review focus
- Explainable decision support

## Human Control

AI cannot approve or reject a case automatically. Every final outcome is explicitly submitted by a human reviewer.

## Business Impact

- Faster structured review
- Reduced manual analysis effort
- Better evidence visibility
- Improved consistency
- Human accountability
- Transparent AI usage
- Auditable decisions

## Responsible AI

- Evidence-grounded recommendations
- Uncertainty communication
- No automatic final decisions
- Human override
- Audit trail
- Prompt-injection resistance

## Three Keywords

**Explain** · **Empower** · **Decide**
